About Shield

GWS Shield is an advanced browser extension that provides real-time protection against Microsoft 365 phishing attacks. Designed for enterprises and managed service providers, Shield uses sophisticated detection algorithms to identify and block malicious login pages before credentials can be compromised.

The extension integrates seamlessly with existing security workflows, offering centralized management, comprehensive logging.

Created by CyberDrain, delivered by Greenwire Solutions

How It Protects You

🛡️ Real-Time M365 Protection

Analyzes Microsoft 365 login pages instantly to detect phishing attempts and spoofed domains.

🚫 Page Blocking

Automatically blocks confirmed threats before you can interact with them, preventing credential theft.

🔔 Suspicious Login Alerts

Warns you when logging into potentially dangerous websites that mimic legitimate Microsoft 365 login pages.

🌐 Domain Squatting Protection

Identifies typosquatting and similar-looking domains designed to trick you.

📊 Zero Trust Architecture

All analysis happens client-side - your browsing data never leaves your device.

🔄 Always Up-to-Date

Threat intelligence updates automatically to protect against the latest attacks.

Privacy & Transparency

Your privacy matters. This extension:

  • Does not track your browsing history
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Performs all threat analysis locally on your device
  • Only reports threat detections (not normal browsing) to help improve protection
